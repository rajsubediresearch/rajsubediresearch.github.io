About

I am a public health researcher with more than fifteen years of experience applying epidemiological and biostatistical methods across academia, research, and national public health programs. My current work centers on mathematical modeling of infectious diseases, epidemic forecasting, and excess mortality estimation. Earlier in my career I worked on maternal and child health, health disparities, and program evaluation, often with multidisciplinary teams across several countries.
